LanChat is an early preview of an open source Android chat app for people who are near each other. Messages pass from phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Google Nearby Connections. No internet, router or account is needed.

What it does: direct chats, private groups and a public broadcast channel between nearby phones.

Security, stated plainly: direct chats and private groups are encrypted with AES-GCM. The public Nearby Mesh broadcast channel is NOT end-to-end encrypted; its text is only Base64-encoded, so anyone in range can read it. The encryption design has not been independently reviewed, and identity keys are stored in the app's private storage, not yet wrapped by the Android Keystore. Do not rely on it where your safety depends on it.

Status: the 29 unit tests pass in CI, but one test has been intermittently flaky, so some CI runs fail. It has not been tested across many real devices, and mesh range, voice call quality and battery use are not measured. The code was generated with Google AI Studio.

Requirements: Android with Google Play Services (Nearby Connections), Bluetooth, Nearby Wi-Fi, camera and microphone permissions.
